# Frames are the clip's own, counted from 0, read at 1280 pixels across
# (ffmpeg scale=1280:-2:flags=bicubic, grey), the size the editor tracks
# at. `quad` is the surface set on the clip's first frame and `exclude` a
# part of it left out; each `at` line is where the same points on the
# surface are on that frame. The points were followed by patch matching,
# each on its own, checked by eye against the first frame at the last
# line and fitted by one homography per line (under 1.6 px off).
# `limit` is the most mean point error over all lines and the most at the
# last one (drift), in pixels: about twice what the tracker made of it
# when the clip was added.
