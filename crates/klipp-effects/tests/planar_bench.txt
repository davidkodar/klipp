# The planar tracker's real-footage bench (#2754): scripts/planar-bench.sh
# fetches each clip by its address, checks its sha256 and runs
# klipp-effects' planar_bench test over this file. The clips are free
# licence (Coverr License, Mixkit Stock Video Free License) and are never
# kept in the repository.
#
# Frames are the clip's own, counted from 0, read at 1280 pixels across
# (ffmpeg scale=1280:-2:flags=bicubic, grey), the size the editor tracks
# at. `quad` is the surface set on the clip's first frame and `exclude` a
# part of it left out; each `at` line is where the same points on the
# surface are on that frame. The points were followed by patch matching,
# each on its own, checked by eye against the first frame at the last
# line and fitted by one homography per line (under 1.6 px off).
# `limit` is the most mean point error over all lines and the most at the
# last one (drift), in pixels: about twice what the tracker made of it
# when the clip was added. `known` marks a clip the tracker can't hold
# yet: reported, not failed, until it holds within its limit, and then
# failed until the line is taken off. `loss_from` is the frame from which
# the surface may be lost: a frame checked there that the tracker reports
# lost (or stopped at after a loss) counts as reported, not drifted, and
# is left out of the errors; before it, a lost frame is an error.
