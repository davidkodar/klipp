# More on the ops

Fields, limits and examples op by op, for the ops above that have more to them.

Editorial operations, resolved by Klipp into frame-exact edits. Give a list of ops; each is an object with "op" and its fields:

place {media_id, track, at, source_in?, source_out?, insert?, with_audio?, sync_source_secs?} — a media item onto a track (an index from klipp_timeline, or "video"/"audio"/"text" for the first of that kind) at an anchor; source_in/out in seconds of the file, the whole file by default; for still images this is the hold range and may exceed the import duration. Its sound comes along linked on the first audio track (made if none) unless with_audio is false (B-roll over a voice); an empty sequence takes the first picture's size and rate; with insert, every unlocked track is rippled later to make room and a clip straddling the anchor is split. sync_source_secs is the moment of the file (seconds from its start; search_sfx's heard.peak_secs) that lands on at: the clip starts that much earlier, to the sample on an audio track, so a whoosh's peak hits the cut; refused when the moment is outside the stretch placed or the clip would start before 0 (the reply gives the source_in that fits).

add_track {kind, name?} — a video or text track on top (it becomes track 0, every other index moves down by one), an audio or subtitle track at the bottom.

remove_track {track, with_clips?} — a track out (index or kind), every track below moving up by one; refused while it holds clips unless with_clips, which takes them and unlinks their partners elsewhere.
set_track {track, visible?, language?, locked?} — a track's own settings, one undo step: visible false hides its picture or captions (its eye), true shows it; language a BCP 47 tag (de, pt-br; empty for none) that a subtitle export tags its file with; locked true keeps every edit off its clips (an insert or a cut leaves a music bed whole), false lets them be edited again. A translated caption track (klipp_translate_captions) starts hidden so the two languages don't draw over each other: show it, and hide its source, to see it.
edit_from_selects {from, from_secs, to_secs, at?, overwrite?} — what lies from from_secs to to_secs in another sequence (from: its id or name) put into the open one at at (its In point when left out): inserted with every track moved along, or overwritten; one undo; refused if that sequence changed before it lands.
track_automation {track, param?, mode?, keys?:[{at, db} | {at, pan}]} — an audio track's own automation, one undo step: mode off, read (the default), touch, latch or write; keys for param volume (dB, 0 unity, up to +6, -60 or below silence) or pan (-1 left to 1 right) at sequence seconds, straight moves between, replacing that control's keys (empty clears them). The keys stay put when clips move, so a fade-out across several clips is one lane: {param: volume, keys: [{at: 58, db: 0}, {at: 62, db: -60}]}.

captions {track?, from?, to?, max_chars?, max_lines?, replace?} — captions for what is heard on the timeline, from the transcripts (run klipp_analyze first): each word lands where its clip puts it, cues close at sentences, pauses and cuts, two lines of 42 by default; on the first subtitle track, made at the bottom if there is none. The reply lists every cue to be read; correct one with set_text. Do it after the speech is placed and trimmed; after a re-cut, make them again with replace true.

audio {clip_id, volume_db?, gain_db?, pan?, fade_in?, fade_out?} — a clip's sound: volume and pre-fader gain in dB, pan -1..1, fades in seconds; what is left out stays. For an audio clip linked to a video clip the settings go on the video clip, where playback and the render read them. track (index or kind) in place of clip_id, with from?/to?, sets every clip heard on it in one step.

clip_audio {clip_id, effect, params?: {name: value}, bypass?, remove?} — as track_audio, on one clip's own chain (a linked pair's, either clip named). Its reverb, delay and chorus ring on past the clip's out point into a gap or under the next clip, until below −80 dB (at most 10 s), its fades going in before them; playback that starts inside a tail plays it from there.
track_audio {track, effect, params?: {name: value}, bypass?, remove?} — the track's own audio chain, run after every clip's own (eq, compressor, limiter, gate, high_pass, denoiser, voice_isolation, deesser, hum_removal, reverb…): adds the effect (or sets the one there), bypasses it, or takes it off. A param name the effect does not have is refused. A switch (an eq band's _enabled, ping_pong) takes true or false, or 1 and 0. Params by effect: eq band1..band6 _enabled/_frequency/_gain/_q; compressor threshold, ratio, attack, release, makeup, knee; limiter threshold, ceiling, input_gain, output_gain, attack, release, lookahead, mix; gate threshold, attack, release, range; high_pass frequency, slope; denoiser strength, floor, wind, profile_seconds; voice_isolation reduction, mode; reverb room_size, damping, wet, width, pre_delay; delay delay_time, feedback, mix, ping_pong; deesser frequency, threshold (dB of the sibilance band relative to the whole voice, −20 default), reduction, bandwidth (Q, 1 default: about 3.8–9.4 kHz); hum_removal fundamental_hz, harmonics (1 to 5, 5 by default: 50 Hz hum and its harmonics to 250 Hz), q, harmonic_2_enabled, harmonic_3_enabled; noise_reduction reduction (dB a band is turned down), sensitivity (0–100: how far above each band's own noise floor counts as signal, 2 to 12 dB), smoothing (a multi-band gate on the floor between words; a steady sound held longer than about 1.5 s counts as floor); declick threshold, max_click_ms (bridges each click it finds, looking 10 ms ahead); normalize target_lufs, mode (1, the default: the whole clip to target_lufs integrated by one gain, measured from the clip's sound through its chain before the effect, in the background and by the export, none until measured; on a clip only; 0: ride, a leveler toward the target as it plays, on a clip or a track); pitch_shift semitones (−12 to 12, up positive), fine_cents; chorus rate, depth, mix, voices; multiband_compressor xover_low, xover_mid, xover_high, threshold_1..4, ratio_1..4, makeup_1..4, attack, release.

duck {clip_id, by_db?, fade_secs?, bridge_secs?} — music under speech: the clip's volume comes down (9 dB by default) wherever speech is heard over it (not clips marked music, sfx, ambience, foley or broll, nor any set 12 dB under the music; a transcribed clip only where its words are, a voiceover throughout), moving over fade_secs (0.5) each way, down across gaps under bridge_secs (2), as volume keyframes. Set the music's volume first, place the speech first.

volume {clip_id, keys:[{at, db}]} — a clip's volume drawn by hand: levels in dB from the clip's own level (0 as set, -9 a duck, -60 near silence) at timeline seconds, straight moves between; replaces its volume keyframes. For the dip duck cannot find, or a swell.

grade {clip_id | on, exposure?, contrast?, saturation?, temperature?, tint?, lift?, gamma?, gain?, highlights?, shadows?, vibrance?, reset?} — a picture clip's primary grade: what is given is set, the rest stays. Every knob is 0 at neutral except saturation and contrast, which are multipliers with 1 as shot (1.1 a touch more, 0.9 less); exposure in stops; temperature/tint the white balance in mireds of the assumed light, -50 to 50: a unit moves a mid grey about 0.2 Lab b*, so +25 is b* +5 (a clear warm) and +50 is +10 (light assumed at 9600 K against D65's 6500 K: strong); +10 is just visible, a tenth of a unit does nothing; lift/gamma/gain the shadows/mids/highlights; gain and highlights are strong for their range, so a first touch is ±0.02 to 0.05 (on a sunlit shot highlights -0.2 took the white point from 84 to 65 IRE, and gain +0.05 clipped a sixth of the frame): step small and read frame_stats between; vibrance is skin-safe saturation; reset takes it to neutral first. Read frame_stats and grade to the numbers; grade for continuity first — shots that follow each other should match — then the look.

zones {clip_id | on, zone?, exposure?, hue?, amount?, saturation?, boundary?, falloff?, preset?, reset?} — HDR zones: exposure, colour and saturation placed by tone in stops about mid grey, zone is global (everything, the default), black, dark, shadow (reaching down from their boundary: −4.5, −2.5, −1 stops) or light, highlight, specular (reaching up from theirs: +1, +2.5, +4.5 on HDR and log; on an SDR clip new zones start them at +0.5, +1.5, +2.2, about 25%, 51% and 83% of white, and the reply says so; stored zones keep theirs); exposure in stops, −4 to 4; hue in degrees on the colour wheel (0 red, 30 orange, 210 a cool blue) with amount 0 to 1 (1 the rim; a quarter is a clear tint) its colour, which never changes brightness; saturation 1 as shot, 0 to 2; boundary −8 to 8 and falloff 0 to 4 stops move where a zone reaches (not global). preset starts every zone from: HDR highlight roll-off, Lift shadows, Deepen blacks, Midtone contrast, or Warm highlights, cool shadows; reset from neutral; what else is given is set after. One zones node per grade; a second op edits it. For tonal balance by stops without a key: calm HDR highlights, open shadows, split-tone.

look {clip_id | on, lut_path, name?, opacity?, remove?} — the batch op takes a resolved absolute `.cube` file path in lut_path. name is only the display label, not a library lookup. opacity is 0–1 (default 1); remove true clears the look and needs no path. Targets are the same as grade. For a bundled name instead, use the standalone klipp_look tool with name from klipp_looks (or an absolute .cube path in name).

Place and look in one batch (replace MEDIA_ID with an imported video ID, ensure video track 0 has room and the source is at least 4 seconds, and replace the example LUT path with an existing absolute .cube path):

```json
[
  {"op":"place","media_id":"MEDIA_ID","track":0,"at":{"secs":0},"source_in":0,"source_out":4,"with_audio":false},
  {"op":"look","clip_id":"@0","lut_path":"/absolute/path/to/look.cube","name":"My look","opacity":0.25}
]
```

Pass this list as `ops` to klipp_plan, then the same ops to klipp_apply with the returned revision and project identity. `@0` refers to the clip placed by operation 0; it is not a media ID. If only a bundled name is known, place the clip first and call klipp_look on its returned clip ID instead.

grade_group {group, clips:[clip_id…], leave?, look_target?, departs?} — picture clips into a named grade group (made when new; a clip is in one group at most), or out of it with leave; look_target names a preset its shots are graded and judged to instead of the brief's ("none" clears it), departs true judges them for faults only, a section off the look on purpose; either works on a group alone; remove_grade_group {group} ends one, its clips keep their own grades. A group's clips share a pre-clip grade (before each one's own: a camera's or a scene's balance) and a post-clip grade (after it: the scene's look). grade and look take on in place of clip_id: on {group, stage?: "pre"|"post" (default)} grades the group once for every member; on "timeline" grades the whole sequence after every clip. One look over a scene is a group and one look on it, not the same look on every shot.

slip {clip_id, secs} — move what a clip shows, not the clip: source in and out both by secs (later in the file for positive), the timeline untouched; its linked sound slips by as much, a J or L cut kept. slide {clip_id, secs} — the clip moved between its neighbours, its picture and length kept: the one before longer, the one after shorter, sound with them; refused where a neighbour runs out. strobe {clip_id, piece_frames, on?, off?, from?, to?, ripple?} — the clip (or from..to inside it) cut into pieces piece_frames long and of every on pieces kept the next off removed (1 and 1), so the shot flickers; ripple closes the gaps, else they stay black; the last piece takes the remainder; one undo. set_length {clips, frames, keep?, ripple?} — each clip made frames long, keep start (default), end or centre; ripple closes up, as for a rapid-fire montage of 3-frame clips; one undo. over_edits {clip_id, tracks?, align?, every_clip?, from?, to?} — a copy of the clip (a flash, an adjustment clip) on its own track over every cut of the targeted picture tracks (or the tracks given), align centre (default), start or end on the cut; every_clip uses each clip's start; a cut where a copy would overlap is skipped; one undo. role {clip_id, role} — what a clip is to the mix and the checks: dialogue, music, sfx, ambience, foley, voiceover, broll, graphics; a bed marked music is never taken for speech. matte {clip_id, source?, channel?, invert?, feather_px?, choke_px?, show_source?, clear?} — the clip seen only through another (track matte); without source, the clip above it: title {text, size 700-1000, font "Bebas Neue", over_faces: true} over a clip, then matte on the clip, puts the footage inside the word. format {width, height, fps?} — the sequence's picture size (and rate): the delivery's, the picture fitted, never stretched.

redact {clip_id, x, y, w, h, from?, to?, kind?, amount?} — hide a patch of the picture (an email, a key, a face) for a stretch: a cropped copy pixelated (kind mosaic, default) or blurred on a video track above; x, y, w, h are fractions of the frame from the top left, as check's sensitive_on_screen and klipp_screen_text give them; from and to default to the clip's edges. auto_redact does every finding at once.

pin {clip_id, media_id | layer, surface?, from?, to?, fit?, inset?, on_loss?, fade_secs?, light?, blend?, template?} — a picture, video or still onto a surface klipp_track_surface followed in the clip: on a video track above, following it; or layer, a clip already on the timeline (a title, a graphic, a generator), pinned where it is over its own time (no from/to), a title still edited with set_text and its style; on_loss hold|hide|fade (fade_secs, 0.3 when left out) is what it does where the surface's track is lost, hold (the last good place) when left out; template text_on_wall|screen|sign a ready look (the fields given stand over it): text_on_wall fitted in a margin, takes the light, fades out where lost; screen stretched inside a bezel in its own light, hidden where lost; sign fitted in most of the light, fades out; light 0-1 how much it darkens and lightens with the surface against its reference frame (a surface tracked since the light was measured; track it again otherwise); blend normal|multiply|screen|overlay|soft_light; surface is its id or name, the clip's latest when left out; fit stretch|fit (auto by shape when left out); inset a margin each side, ~0.02 for a screen's bezel.

label {clip_id, name?, colour?, flag?} — the clip's own name on the timeline, shown in place of its media's (blank gives the media's back), and its colour: none, red, orange, yellow, green, blue or purple. flag says where its grade stands: none, needs work, review or approved; flag shots to come back to and sign off the finished ones. Timeline and list_clips show all three.

effect {clip_id | on: "timeline" | {group} | {track, from?, to?, itself?}, kind, amount?, params?} — a finishing effect on a picture or a title (on a title it works on the drawn text: blur, glow, directional_blur…), or on every picture of the timeline, a grade group or a track (a text track's titles too) in one step (with itself: true, on the track itself, after every clip's own, clips added later too): vignette, grain, glow, bloom, diffusion, halation, light_rays (params center_x, center_y place its light), lens_flare (params position_x, position_y), anamorphic, blur, radial_blur, directional_blur, sharpen, chromatic_aberration, tilt_shift, mosaic, posterize, invert, mirror, lens_distortion, displace (ripple, heat_haze: pixels moved by a drifting noise field; params amount and size in canvas px, speed, direction both/across/up_down, seed), reflection (water_reflection; surfaces calm_water (default), rippled_water, glossy_floor, wet_street, glass: the layer mirrored about a waterline below it, on the water shot under it; params waterline (frame height fraction, 0.6: the layer's base), gap (canvas px down to the water; 0 rests on it, more floats above), ripple_amount, ripple_size (canvas px), ripple_speed, breakup, smear, fade, opacity, darken, contact (the dark band at the waterline) (0-1), blend normal/screen/soft_light/overlay, tint #rrggbb, tint_amount, submerge (0-1, how far the layer's own pixels below the line are under the water: 1 hides them, 0.7 on water), seed), edge_detect, denoise, vhs, old_film, flicker (random opacity jumps; exposure_flicker for the light), binocular (two eyepieces, a fringe at their rims), dust, snow, rain, sparks, embers (particles over the picture, more with the amount), corners (rounded alpha mask), shadow (drop shadow), outer_glow (glow outside the layer silhouette), fill_bars (the black bars round an upright phone shot in a wide frame filled with the picture, enlarged and blurred; more amount is softer and darker); amount 0-1. These are effect kinds, e.g. {op: effect, clip_id: ID, kind: corners, amount: 0.4}; shadow and outer_glow use the same fields. params {name: value} sets any of the effect's own settings over kind and amount, inside its range, a choice by name, a colour #rrggbb, e.g. {op: effect, clip_id: ID, kind: old_film, params: {scratches: 0, sepia: 1}}; a wrong name is refused with the ones there are. Place the picture with compose to show the backdrop around it. A key is kind chroma_key (green_screen, blue_screen) on one clip, the app's Chroma Key: color #rrggbb (green; blue for blue_screen), tolerance 0.2, softness 0.1, spill 0.6, each 0-1; kind luma_key keys by brightness, the app's Luma Key: low to high keyed out (0, 0.1: black), softness 0.05, each 0-1, invert true keeps that range instead. clean_audio {clip_id, voice?, reduction_db?, wind?, hiss?, hum?, deess?} — clean a clip's sound, each flag its tool: voice isolates dialogue from wind, traffic and room noise with the Voice Isolation AI effect, the best cleanup where someone speaks (reduction_db 12 keeps the place, default 24, 60 removes all it can; leave it off where nobody speaks, it would take the ambience); wind for gusts and rumble, hiss for tape or air hiss, hum for mains buzz, deess for harsh sibilance. opacity_mask {clip_id, shape: {linear: {start: [x,y], end: [x,y]}} | {radial: {center: [x,y], inner, outer}}, feather?, invert?, clear?} — fade a clip's opacity across the frame so the track below shows through: whole at start (inside inner), gone at end (past outer); points are frame fractions, radii frame widths; feather 0-1 how much of the way it fades (1 default, 0 a hard edge midway); invert swaps the sides; a second replaces the first; clear true takes it off; refused where the clip's alpha is already another matte. stabilize {clip_id, smoothing?} — steady a shaky clip (0-1): the shake taken out, a pan or tracking move kept, enlarged only as far as its correction needs. replace {clip_id, media_id, source_in?} — another file in the clip's slot: same place, length and speed, its grade, effects, role, volume, links and transitions kept, its linked sound moved with it; source_in defaults to the clip's own when it fits, else the file's start. retime {clip_id, speed | fit, ripple?, interpolation?} — change speed, its linked sound with it; fit true instead of speed fills the room to the next clip on its track exactly, the speed worked out (fit to fill) (>1 faster/shorter, <1 slower/longer, negative reversed; the sound plays at the speed, its pitch moving with it, backward when reversed; a speed ramp's sound is silent); interpolation sets how a slowed clip's in-between frames are made, alone or with a speed: sample repeats the nearest frame, blend cross-fades the two either side, flow follows the motion between them (smooth slow motion; where too much can't be followed it cross-fades); ripple moves the clips after it, without it a clip that would grow into the next is refused and a shrunk one leaves a gap. generator {data, at, secs?|end?, track?} — place native generator data (see klipp_generator), with exactly one duration secs or end anchor. Omit track for a Background video track below all existing layers; e.g. {op: generator, data: {SolidColor: {color: [16,24,32,255]}}, at: {secs: 0}, secs: 3}.

compose {clip_id, poses: [{secs? | from_end_secs?, x?, y?, width?, height?, rotation_deg?, tilt_x_deg?, tilt_y_deg?, opacity?, ease?}], flip_x?, flip_y?} — set the clip transform for picture-in-picture, split screens or grids; one pose holds, more animate. The clip is a media clip, a generator or a title; a title moves, scales and turns whole, every text and shape in it together, which stay editable, and keeps its own motion (each text's enter, exit and keyed moves) under the clip's. For a title, x and y place the centre of the whole frame it is drawn on, not of its text: x 0.2 puts a centred title's text at 0.2 of the width. On a clip panned inside its card it moves the card and keeps the pan; the card then takes one place (poses may still fade it). secs is relative to the clip, whose ends sit on frames (it may be up to a frame shorter than asked; a pose up to a frame past its end is its end); from_end_secs instead counts back from the end (0 is the end); x,y are its centre as frame fractions (-1..2, default 0.5); width and height are each 0.01..4 (default 1; a height left out takes the width, and unequal ones stretch the picture, except on a clip panned inside its card); rotation_deg is -360..360 (default 0), tilt_x_deg and tilt_y_deg are -80..80 degrees (default 0), opacity 0..1 (default 1). flip_x and flip_y (true or false) mirror the whole clip left to right or top to bottom about its anchor, or take a flip off; left out, it stays as it is; they need no pose change, but a compose still needs one pose. Example: {op: compose, clip_id: ID, poses: [{x: 0.7, y: 0.7, width: 0.4, height: 0.4}]}; mirrored upside down in place: {op: compose, clip_id: ID, poses: [{}], flip_y: true}. ease is how the move into a pose eases (see Easing below; in_out when left out).

camera {keys: [{secs?, x?, y?, zoom?, roll_deg?, ease?}]} — move the whole sequence, including all its layers. secs is TIMELINE time within the piece (default 0); x,y are the camera's look point (-1..2, default 0.5), zoom 0.1..8 (default 1), roll_deg -360..360 (default 0). One key holds; keys: [] clears the camera. Example over a three-second piece: {op: camera, keys: [{secs: 0}, {secs: 3, zoom: 1.1, x: 0.55}]}.

reframe {clip_id, scale?, x?, y?} — punch in and reposition a picture: scale zooms (1 as shot, up to 4), x and y pan -1..1. Salvage a wide or badly-framed shot, or set a slow push; it sits before other effects.

zoom {clip_id, from?, to, x?, y?, ease?} — an ANIMATED zoom over the clip: scale from→to (1 as shot, up to 4), a push-in or pull-out, optionally moving toward x,y (-1..1) for a Ken Burns move; ease is linear when left out, so a drift keeps its speed. Renders on the GPU; motion on a still shot.

pan {clip_id, keys: [{secs, scale?, x?, y?, ease?}]} — an animated crop through poses in the clip's own seconds: scale (1-4) centred on frame point x,y (0..1, as the beat sheet's spots and screen text give them); eases between (in_out when left out), never past the edge. On a picture composed as a card (smaller than the frame, moved, or cropped) it pans inside the card: the card keeps its place and size, and x,y are points across the card; it keys the clip's crop, adding one when there is none. A card that moves is refused: compose it with one pose, pan, then compose again, which moves the card and keeps the pan inside it.

Easing (compose poses, pan keys, camera keys, zoom): ease is the curve of the move INTO that key from the one before: linear (constant speed, starts and stops dead), in (starts slowly, arrives at speed), out (leaves at speed and settles), in_out (slow at both ends), expo_out (a fast arrival that settles long), back_out (overshoots a little and settles back). compose, pan and camera default to in_out: each key is a rest, the move stopping and starting there, so keys close together (a few tenths of a second apart) pulse at that rate. For one continuous move through several points, give every key after the first ease: linear, e.g. {op: pan, clip_id: ID, keys: [{secs: 0, scale: 1}, {secs: 2, scale: 1.15, x: 0.45, y: 0.5, ease: linear}, {secs: 4, scale: 1.3, x: 0.4, y: 0.48, ease: linear}]}; in_out on the last key alone lets it settle at the end.

follow {clip_id, scale?, hold_secs?} — a pan made from the beat sheet: push to each spot where something happened in the clip's stretch, hold, ease back out.

highlight {at, secs?, x, y, w, h, kind? box|ring|spot, color?, stroke?, track?} — a highlight over the region (centre x,y, size w,h, fractions of the frame): a rounded box, a capsule ring, or everything outside it dimmed; fades in and out.

freeze {clip_id, at, secs} — a freeze frame: the clip's picture at at held for secs (a frame to 60), inserted there, everything from at on, on every unlocked track, moved on by secs; the held piece keeps the clip's grade and effects, its sound silent; trim it to change how long it holds. ramp {clip_id, from, to, secs?, ripple?, interpolation?} — a speed ramp from×→to× over secs of timeline, then holding to; the clip's length follows: ripple moves the clips after it, without it a ramp that would grow into the next clip is refused; interpolation sample/blend/flow as retime's, for the slow part's frames.

qualify {clip_id, target?|hue?|rgb?|keep?, take_out?, hue_width?, sat_low?, sat_high?, lum_low?, lum_high?, invert?, exposure?, saturation?, hue_shift?, temperature?, remove?, window?, window_feather?} — a secondary: key a colour or tone (target skin/sky/foliage/reds…, or a hue in degrees, with saturation and luminance windows; or rgb [red_low, red_high, green_low, green_high, blue_low, blue_high], 0-1 on the picture as shown, for a colour hue separates poorly; or keep, colours [[r, g, b], …] 0-1 sampled across what to key, less take_out's: the colour volume they span, cleanest on skin and skies) and correct only it (exposure, saturation, hue_shift, temperature, in mireds as the grade's: +25 a clear warm); invert grades all but the key. window holds the key inside a shape as well — a list of {at, x, y, w, h}, boxes in frame fractions at times in clip seconds — because a hue key alone cannot tell a face from a red jumper, a brick wall or a wooden table, and turns all of them. Several entries keyframe the window so it follows a subject who moves. Fix a sky, calm a loud shirt, warm skin.

transition {clip_id, kind?, secs?, pieces?, color?} — a transition between a clip and the one after it on its track, secs long (0.5 default), centred on the cut. kind is cross_dissolve (the default, and all an interview or documentary wants), dip_to_black, dip_to_white, or a stylised one (zoom, whip, glitch, film_burn, or the graphic piece transitions strips, panels, halves, shatter) a piece asks for by its style, never by default. color (#rrggbb) is the colour of colorize_flash, light_leak or fade_to_color. smooth_cut hides a jump cut between two takes of one framing, e.g. where words were cut from an interview: the last frame before the cut morphs into the first after it, the picture holding still meanwhile, so keep it short (0.2-0.4 s); it needs no footage past the cut, and with framing analysis it is refused where the faces move or change size too much between the two. pieces sets how strips, panels or halves move: {count, angle (degrees), stagger (0-0.9), order (in_sequence, reversed, centre_out, edges_in, random, all_at_once), ease (smooth, linear, accelerate, overshoot), assemble (the next shot's pieces arrive), alternate, gap (px), shear (halves slide along the line), motion (slide, tilt, turn, fall, shrink), shadow, seed (0-255), subject ({motion: stays, steps_forward or goes_first, model: subject or foreground}: the subject cut out as its own piece by a tracked matte, the outgoing shot's or, assembling, the incoming one's; null for none)}, each optional, the rest as the kind has it. A subject needs its matte: klipp_track_subject with the transition_id, and an export waits for it. Both clips need footage beyond the cut to overlap. On audio, a crossfade.

style {clip_id | track, from?, to?, size?, color?, font?, bold?, align?, x?, y?, backing?, outline?, full_frame?, shadow?} — the look and place of one title, or of every title on a track ("subtitle" for all the captions), between from and to when given: size in pixels of a 1080-line frame, color #rrggbb, x and y where the text's centre goes (0 to 1), backing how dark a panel sits behind it (0 none to 1 black), outline a width in pixels. It is how captions are lifted clear of a lower third while it is up.

set_text {clip_id, text} — the words of a title or a caption, its look, place and timing kept: for a cue the transcript misheard or a name it cannot spell.

trim {clip_id, edge: "in"|"out", to, ripple?, linked?} — one edge to an anchor, the other stays, the source moves with the edge; the clip it is linked to (a picture's sound) is trimmed with it by the same amount unless linked is false; with ripple the later clips on the track close up by the change (a head trim then keeps the clip's start).

split {clip_id, at} — into two, the linked sound with it.

join {clip_id, side?} — close a through edit: the clip and the piece of the same shot on its side (next, default, or previous) made one again, its sound with it; name the false cut's own neighbours, as every edit of one film runs on in its file.

remove {clip_id, ripple?, linked?} — off the track, its linked sound or picture with it unless linked is false; with ripple the later clips close the gap; a linked ripple refuses different timeline ranges or a locked partner. Use ripple:false to leave later clips in place.

cut {from, to} — that stretch out of every unlocked track, linked halves split with it, everything after moved up: a text-based delete. Refused through a retimed clip.

move {clip_id, to} — the clip's start to an anchor, length unchanged, a linked partner with it.

j_cut {incoming_clip, frames} — the incoming clip's sound starts so many frames before its picture; l_cut {outgoing_clip, frames} — the outgoing clip's sound stays so many frames past its picture. Both need linked audio on one track.

Anchors: {"secs": s}, {"frame": n}, {"clip_start": clip}, {"clip_end": clip}, {"track_end": track} (where the last clip on a track ends: where an assembly's next select goes), {"marker": label}, {"offset": {"from": anchor, "secs": s}}. A clip in an anchor, a clip_id (and incoming_clip, outgoing_clip) is an id string, or {"track": n, "secs": s} for the clip covering that time on that track as the plan stands at that step — how a later step names a half a split made or a clip a place put down.

Ops run in order, each seeing the ones before it (a split's halves are new ids, reported in the reply). Times snap to frames. Overlaps, locked tracks and edges past the media are refused with why and what would help, and nothing is applied.

match {clip_id, keep?} — the clip takes the start and length of the clip under it: the one it overlaps most on the nearest track below that has one, else above, never its own linked sound. Done through move and set_length, so it is refused where it would run over a neighbour or past its source. With keep true it stays matched: when the shot is trimmed, rolled or moved the clip follows in the same undo step, and the link is let go (the clip left where it is) when the shot is deleted, when the clip is moved or trimmed on its own, or when following would be refused. keep false, or left out, matches once and stops a clip kept matched being kept so. A split or copied clip is not kept matched.
