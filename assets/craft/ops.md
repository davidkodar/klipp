# More on the ops

Fields, limits and examples op by op, for the ops above that have more to them.

Editorial operations, resolved by Klipp into frame-exact edits. Give a list of ops; each is an object with "op" and its fields:

place {media_id, track, at, source_in?, source_out?, insert?, with_audio?} — a media item onto a track (an index from klipp_timeline, or "video"/"audio"/"text" for the first of that kind) at an anchor; source_in/out in seconds of the file, the whole file by default; for still images this is the hold range and may exceed the import duration. Its sound comes along linked on the first audio track (made if none) unless with_audio is false (B-roll over a voice); an empty sequence takes the first picture's size and rate; with insert, every unlocked track is rippled later to make room and a clip straddling the anchor is split.

add_track {kind, name?} — a video or text track on top (it becomes track 0, every other index moves down by one), an audio or subtitle track at the bottom.

remove_track {track, with_clips?} — a track out (index or kind), every track below moving up by one; refused while it holds clips unless with_clips, which takes them and unlinks their partners elsewhere.

captions {track?, from?, to?, max_chars?, max_lines?, replace?} — captions for what is heard on the timeline, from the transcripts (run klipp_analyze first): each word lands where its clip puts it, cues close at sentences, pauses and cuts, two lines of 42 by default; on the first subtitle track, made at the bottom if there is none. The reply lists every cue to be read; correct one with set_text. Do it after the speech is placed and trimmed; after a re-cut, make them again with replace true.

audio {clip_id, volume_db?, gain_db?, pan?, fade_in?, fade_out?} — a clip's sound: volume and pre-fader gain in dB, pan -1..1, fades in seconds; what is left out stays. For an audio clip linked to a video clip the settings go on the video clip, where playback and the render read them. track (index or kind) in place of clip_id, with from?/to?, sets every clip heard on it in one step.

track_audio {track, effect, params?: {name: value}, bypass?, remove?} — the track's own audio chain, run after every clip's own (eq, compressor, limiter, gate, high_pass, denoiser, voice_isolation, deesser, hum_removal, reverb…): adds the effect (or sets the one there), bypasses it, or takes it off.

duck {clip_id, by_db?, fade_secs?, bridge_secs?} — music under speech: the clip's volume comes down (9 dB by default) wherever speech is heard over it (not clips marked music, sfx, ambience, foley or broll, nor any set 12 dB under the music; a transcribed clip only where its words are, a voiceover throughout), moving over fade_secs (0.5) each way, down across gaps under bridge_secs (2), as volume keyframes. Set the music's volume first, place the speech first.

volume {clip_id, keys:[{at, db}]} — a clip's volume drawn by hand: levels in dB from the clip's own level (0 as set, -9 a duck, -60 near silence) at timeline seconds, straight moves between; replaces its volume keyframes. For the dip duck cannot find, or a swell.

grade {clip_id | on, exposure?, contrast?, saturation?, temperature?, tint?, lift?, gamma?, gain?, highlights?, shadows?, vibrance?, reset?} — a picture clip's primary grade: what is given is set, the rest stays. Every knob is 0 at neutral except saturation and contrast, which are multipliers with 1 as shot (1.1 a touch more, 0.9 less); exposure in stops; temperature/tint the white balance in mireds, -50 to 50 (about 5 Lab b* at 25; a tenth of a unit does nothing); lift/gamma/gain the shadows/mids/highlights; gain and highlights are strong for their range, so a first touch is ±0.02 to 0.05 (on a sunlit shot highlights -0.2 took the white point from 84 to 65 IRE, and gain +0.05 clipped a sixth of the frame): step small and read frame_stats between; vibrance is skin-safe saturation; reset takes it to neutral first. Read frame_stats and grade to the numbers; grade for continuity first — shots that follow each other should match — then the look.

grade_group {group, clips:[clip_id…], leave?, look_target?, departs?} — picture clips into a named grade group (made when new; a clip is in one group at most), or out of it with leave; look_target names a preset its shots are graded and judged to instead of the brief's ("none" clears it), departs true judges them for faults only, a section off the look on purpose; either works on a group alone; remove_grade_group {group} ends one, its clips keep their own grades. A group's clips share a pre-clip grade (before each one's own: a camera's or a scene's balance) and a post-clip grade (after it: the scene's look). grade and look take on in place of clip_id: on {group, stage?: "pre"|"post" (default)} grades the group once for every member; on "timeline" grades the whole sequence after every clip. One look over a scene is a group and one look on it, not the same look on every shot.

slip {clip_id, secs} — move what a clip shows, not the clip: source in and out both by secs (later in the file for positive), the timeline untouched; its linked sound slips by as much, a J or L cut kept. slide {clip_id, secs} — the clip moved between its neighbours, its picture and length kept: the one before longer, the one after shorter, sound with them; refused where a neighbour runs out. strobe {clip_id, piece_frames, on?, off?, from?, to?, ripple?} — the clip (or from..to inside it) cut into pieces piece_frames long and of every on pieces kept the next off removed (1 and 1), so the shot flickers; ripple closes the gaps, else they stay black; the last piece takes the remainder; one undo. set_length {clips, frames, keep?, ripple?} — each clip made frames long, keep start (default), end or centre; ripple closes up, as for a rapid-fire montage of 3-frame clips; one undo. over_edits {clip_id, tracks?, align?, every_clip?, from?, to?} — a copy of the clip (a flash, an adjustment clip) on its own track over every cut of the targeted picture tracks (or the tracks given), align centre (default), start or end on the cut; every_clip uses each clip's start; a cut where a copy would overlap is skipped; one undo. role {clip_id, role} — what a clip is to the mix and the checks: dialogue, music, sfx, ambience, foley, voiceover, broll, graphics; a bed marked music is never taken for speech. matte {clip_id, source?, channel?, invert?, feather_px?, choke_px?, show_source?, clear?} — the clip seen only through another (track matte); without source, the clip above it: title {text, size 700-1000, font "Bebas Neue", over_faces: true} over a clip, then matte on the clip, puts the footage inside the word. format {width, height, fps?} — the sequence's picture size (and rate): the delivery's, the picture fitted, never stretched.

redact {clip_id, x, y, w, h, from?, to?, kind?, amount?} — hide a patch of the picture (an email, a key, a face) for a stretch: a cropped copy pixelated (kind mosaic, default) or blurred on a video track above; x, y, w, h are fractions of the frame from the top left, as check's sensitive_on_screen and klipp_screen_text give them; from and to default to the clip's edges. auto_redact does every finding at once.

pin {clip_id, media_id, surface?, from?, to?, fit?, inset?} — a picture, video or still onto a surface klipp_track_surface followed in the clip: on a video track above, following it; surface is its id or name, the clip's latest when left out; fit stretch|fit (auto by shape when left out); inset a margin each side, ~0.02 for a screen's bezel.

label {clip_id, name?, colour?, flag?} — the clip's own name on the timeline, shown in place of its media's (blank gives the media's back), and its colour: none, red, orange, yellow, green, blue or purple. flag says where its grade stands: none, needs work, review or approved; flag shots to come back to and sign off the finished ones. Timeline and list_clips show all three.

effect {clip_id | on: "timeline" | {group} | {track, from?, to?, itself?}, kind, amount?} — a finishing effect on a picture, or on every picture of the timeline, a grade group or a track in one step (with itself: true, on the track itself, after every clip's own, clips added later too): vignette, grain, glow, bloom, diffusion, halation, light_rays, lens_flare, anamorphic, blur, radial_blur, directional_blur, sharpen, chromatic_aberration, tilt_shift, mosaic, posterize, invert, mirror, lens_distortion, edge_detect, denoise, vhs, old_film, flicker (random opacity jumps; exposure_flicker for the light), binocular (two eyepieces, a fringe at their rims), corners (rounded alpha mask), shadow (drop shadow), outer_glow (glow outside the layer silhouette), fill_bars (the black bars round an upright phone shot in a wide frame filled with the picture, enlarged and blurred: Resolve's blanking fill; more amount is softer and darker); amount 0-1. These are effect kinds, e.g. {op: effect, clip_id: ID, kind: corners, amount: 0.4}; shadow and outer_glow use the same fields. Place the picture with compose to show the backdrop around it. A key is kind chroma_key (green_screen, blue_screen) on one clip, the app's Chroma Key: color #rrggbb (green; blue for blue_screen), tolerance 0.2, softness 0.1, spill 0.6, each 0-1; kind luma_key keys by brightness, the app's Luma Key: low to high keyed out (0, 0.1: black), softness 0.05, each 0-1, invert true keeps that range instead. clean_audio {clip_id, voice?, reduction_db?, wind?, hiss?, hum?, deess?} — clean a clip's sound, each flag its tool: voice isolates dialogue from wind, traffic and room noise with the Voice Isolation AI effect, the best cleanup where someone speaks (reduction_db 12 keeps the place, default 24, 60 removes all it can; leave it off where nobody speaks, it would take the ambience); wind for gusts and rumble, hiss for tape or air hiss, hum for mains buzz, deess for harsh sibilance. stabilize {clip_id, smoothing?} — steady a shaky clip (0-1, crops a little). replace {clip_id, media_id, source_in?} — another file in the clip's slot: same place, length and speed, its grade, effects, role, volume, links and transitions kept, its linked sound moved with it; source_in defaults to the clip's own when it fits, else the file's start. retime {clip_id, speed | fit, ripple?} — change speed, its linked sound with it; fit true instead of speed fills the room to the next clip on its track exactly, the speed worked out (fit to fill) (>1 faster/shorter, <1 slower/longer, negative reversed; the sound plays at the speed, its pitch moving with it, backward when reversed; a speed ramp's sound is silent); ripple moves the clips after it, without it a clip that would grow into the next is refused and a shrunk one leaves a gap. generator {data, at, secs?|end?, track?} — place native generator data (see klipp_generator), with exactly one duration secs or end anchor. Omit track for a Background video track below all existing layers; e.g. {op: generator, data: {SolidColor: {color: [16,24,32,255]}}, at: {secs: 0}, secs: 3}.

compose {clip_id, poses: [{secs?, x?, y?, width?, height?, rotation_deg?, opacity?}]} — place a picture inside the frame; one pose holds, more animate. secs is relative to the clip; x,y are its centre as frame fractions (-1..2, default 0.5); width and height must be equal (0.01..4, default 1); rotation_deg is -360..360 (default 0), opacity 0..1 (default 1). Example: {op: compose, clip_id: ID, poses: [{x: 0.7, y: 0.7, width: 0.4, height: 0.4}]}.

camera {keys: [{secs?, x?, y?, zoom?, roll_deg?}]} — move the whole sequence, including all its layers. secs is TIMELINE time within the piece (default 0); x,y are the camera's look point (-1..2, default 0.5), zoom 0.1..8 (default 1), roll_deg -360..360 (default 0). One key holds; keys: [] clears the camera. Example over a three-second piece: {op: camera, keys: [{secs: 0}, {secs: 3, zoom: 1.1, x: 0.55}]}.

reframe {clip_id, scale?, x?, y?} — punch in and reposition a picture: scale zooms (1 as shot, up to 4), x and y pan -1..1. Salvage a wide or badly-framed shot, or set a slow push; it sits before other effects.

zoom {clip_id, from?, to, x?, y?} — an ANIMATED zoom over the clip: scale from→to (1 as shot, up to 4), a push-in or pull-out, optionally moving toward x,y (-1..1) for a Ken Burns move. Renders on the GPU; motion on a still shot.

pan {clip_id, keys: [{secs, scale?, x?, y?}]} — an animated crop through poses in the clip's own seconds: scale (1-4) centred on frame point x,y (0..1, as the beat sheet's spots and screen text give them); eases between, never past the edge.

follow {clip_id, scale?, hold_secs?} — a pan made from the beat sheet: push to each spot where something happened in the clip's stretch, hold, ease back out.

highlight {at, secs?, x, y, w, h, kind? box|ring|spot, color?, stroke?, track?} — a highlight over the region (centre x,y, size w,h, fractions of the frame): a rounded box, a capsule ring, or everything outside it dimmed; fades in and out.

ramp {clip_id, from, to, secs?} — a speed ramp from×→to× over secs of timeline, then holding to; the clip's length follows.

qualify {clip_id, target?|hue?|rgb?, hue_width?, sat_low?, sat_high?, lum_low?, lum_high?, invert?, exposure?, saturation?, hue_shift?, temperature?, remove?, window?, window_feather?} — a secondary: key a colour or tone (target skin/sky/foliage/reds…, or a hue in degrees, with saturation and luminance windows; or rgb [red_low, red_high, green_low, green_high, blue_low, blue_high], 0-1 on the picture as shown, for a colour hue separates poorly) and correct only it (exposure, saturation, hue_shift, temperature); invert grades all but the key. window holds the key inside a shape as well — a list of {at, x, y, w, h}, boxes in frame fractions at times in clip seconds — because a hue key alone cannot tell a face from a red jumper, a brick wall or a wooden table, and turns all of them. Several entries keyframe the window so it follows a subject who moves. Fix a sky, calm a loud shirt, warm skin.

transition {clip_id, kind?, secs?} — a transition between a clip and the one after it on its track, secs long (0.5 default), centred on the cut. kind is cross_dissolve (the default, and all an interview or documentary wants), dip_to_black, dip_to_white, or a stylised one (zoom, whip, glitch, film_burn) a piece asks for by its style, never by default. Both clips need footage beyond the cut to overlap. On audio, a crossfade.

style {clip_id | track, from?, to?, size?, color?, font?, bold?, align?, x?, y?, backing?, outline?, full_frame?, shadow?} — the look and place of one title, or of every title on a track ("subtitle" for all the captions), between from and to when given: size in pixels of a 1080-line frame, color #rrggbb, x and y where the text's centre goes (0 to 1), backing how dark a panel sits behind it (0 none to 1 black), outline a width in pixels. It is how captions are lifted clear of a lower third while it is up.

template {name, at, secs?, track?, fields?} — see klipp_title_templates lower_third {name, role?, at, secs?, track?, side?, x?, y?} — a name with a role under it, bottom left in title-safe, four seconds by default, fading in and out, on a text track with room (made on top if none: every other index then moves down by one). Refused across a shot change. Names come from the brief, never a guess. Where the speaker is not on the left or the picture has its own name burnt in there, side "right" sets it against the right edge, x is that edge and y the name's line, as fractions of the frame inside title-safe.

vector_path {clip_id, object_id?, path:{pose:{contours:[{closed,vertices:[{point:[x,y],incoming:[x,y],outgoing:[x,y]}]}],fill:[r,g,b,a],stroke:[r,g,b,a],stroke_width,stroke_glow?:{radius,strength}},keyframes:[{time_secs,pose,easing}],trim?:{range:{start,end,offset},keyframes:[{time_secs,range,easing}]}}} edits a title layer; trim uses independent normalized arc-length keys per contour, open paths clip and closed paths wrap offset, fill unchanged, round caps/joins, glow from revealed stroke only; coordinates normalized to its box, keyed topology must match. title {text, at, style? (a brief type style or heading|callout|card), secs?, track?, x?, y?, size?, color?, font?, bold?, align?, backing?, backing_color?, full_frame?, outline?, outline_color?, glow?, glow_color?, gradient?, animate?, animate_secs?, stagger?, enter?, exit?, loop?, glitch?, chromatic?, shadow?} — a title from scratch, enter/exit are {kind, duration_secs?, delay_secs?, easing?, stagger?, stagger_secs?, distance?, scale?, anchor?}. Kinds include mask_rise/mask_drop and none (disable); easing is linear, out_cubic, out_quint, out_expo, out_back, out_bounce, in_out_cubic, in_cubic or cubic-bezier(x1,y1,x2,y2), over the motion's own time (in_cubic starts slowly on an exit too; an exit's default is in_cubic). Timing: duration (0,3600] s, delay [0,3600] s, stagger [0,60] s; distance [0,100] font-size units, only for rise/drop/slide_left/slide_right. Exits grow and shrink scale without fading, by scale (a factor of the resting size: grow above 1, default 4; shrink below 1, default 0): a word matte whose exit is grow with scale 30 and anchor "solid" flies into a letter until the picture inside fills the frame, for a seamless cut to the full shot. anchor (grow, shrink, scale_up, scale_down, pop, slam, focus_in) is what they scale about: "solid" (the point deepest inside the letters; without it a two-line word grows into the gap between its lines and goes black), "centre", or {x, y} in the title's box. scale also sets how far scale_up (below 1) and scale_down (above 1) go; both fade. Entrance delay plus exit lead must leave a visible interval inside the title. Timing-only overrides on a disabled entrance are ignored. Exit delay is lead time before clip end; exit stagger runs in the renderer's reverse unit order. Masked travel stays fixed. Do not combine enter with animate/animate_secs/stagger. Explicit enter/exit replaces that style side; omitted sides inherit. any look and motion: x,y centre (0..1), size in px of a 1080 frame, color #rrggbb; backing a dark panel (0..1) or backing_color, full_frame a solid card, outline+outline_color a stroke, glow+glow_color a neon glow, shadow {x?, y?, blur?, color?, opacity?} a soft drop shadow (px of a 1080 frame; opacity 0 removes it), gradient [#top,#bottom] or {stops: [[pos 0-1, #rrggbb], ...], angle? (degrees, 0 top to bottom, 90 left to right) | radial? {x, y, r}}; animate an entrance (fade, rise, drop, slide_left, slide_right, scale_up, scale_down, blur, mask_rise, mask_drop, typewriter, pop, slam, fly_left, fly_right, fly_top, fly_bottom, bounce, flicker, focus_in, soft_rise) with stagger (line/word/char); loop a continuous effect (pulse, flash, neon, wave, shake, color_cycle, scroll for a credits roll, crawl for a ticker; roll_shape {focus {band, edge_opacity}} thins a roll away from the middle; edge_fade {top, bottom, left, right} fades any title out towards those frame edges); glitch and chromatic (0..1) for a digital edge. animators (presets wave, random_fade_in, spin_in, or a range selector), keys (size, tracking, line_spacing, color, gradient_stops over time) on_path (a path layer of the title), blend (a blend mode), mask (another layer of the title) and three_d (3D text: a look, depth, bevels, turn, light): klipp_guide topic guide, under title.

set_text {clip_id, text} — the words of a title or a caption, its look, place and timing kept: for a cue the transcript misheard or a name it cannot spell.

trim {clip_id, edge: "in"|"out", to, ripple?, linked?} — one edge to an anchor, the other stays, the source moves with the edge; the clip it is linked to (a picture's sound) is trimmed with it by the same amount unless linked is false; with ripple the later clips on the track close up by the change (a head trim then keeps the clip's start).

split {clip_id, at} — into two, the linked sound with it.

join {clip_id, side?} — close a through edit: the clip and the piece of the same shot on its side (next, default, or previous) made one again, its sound with it; name the false cut's own neighbours, as every edit of one film runs on in its file.

remove {clip_id, ripple?, linked?} — off the track, its linked sound or picture with it unless linked is false; with ripple the later clips close the gap; a linked ripple refuses different timeline ranges or a locked partner. Use ripple:false to leave later clips in place.

cut {from, to} — that stretch out of every unlocked track, linked halves split with it, everything after moved up: a text-based delete. Refused through a retimed clip.

move {clip_id, to} — the clip's start to an anchor, length unchanged, a linked partner with it.

j_cut {incoming_clip, frames} — the incoming clip's sound starts so many frames before its picture; l_cut {outgoing_clip, frames} — the outgoing clip's sound stays so many frames past its picture. Both need linked audio on one track.

Anchors: {"secs": s}, {"frame": n}, {"clip_start": clip}, {"clip_end": clip}, {"track_end": track} (where the last clip on a track ends: where an assembly's next select goes), {"marker": label}, {"offset": {"from": anchor, "secs": s}}. A clip in an anchor, a clip_id (and incoming_clip, outgoing_clip) is an id string, or {"track": n, "secs": s} for the clip covering that time on that track as the plan stands at that step — how a later step names a half a split made or a clip a place put down.

Ops run in order, each seeing the ones before it (a split's halves are new ids, reported in the reply). Times snap to frames. Overlaps, locked tracks and edges past the media are refused with why and what would help, and nothing is applied.
