# Other Pages

The preference pages not covered on their own pages: [General](./general.md), [System](./system.md), [Appearance](./appearance.md), [Audio and Audio Hardware](./audio.md) and [Keyboard](./shortcuts.md).

## Playback

- **Playback quality** — full, half or quarter resolution while playing
- **Background render** — pre-render complex sections in the background; **Background performance**: defer (recommended), balanced or max throughput
- **Optimised playback** — auto (recommended), always the original media, or always the optimised media
- **Pre-roll duration**, **JKL speed steps**, **Loop playback by default**, **Dim UI during playback**
- **Audio scrubbing** — play audio while dragging the playhead

## Editing

- **Timeline behaviour** — snap threshold, grid snap, the snap targets (clip edges, playhead, markers, in/out, grid), zoom step, playhead follows playback, magnetic timeline
- **Defaults** — new sequence resolution, default transition and its duration, the **Shift+arrow step**
- **Multicam** — angle badges on the timeline, and the default sync method: timecode then sound, timecode, or sound
- **Tracking** — search radius, patch size and loss threshold for point tracking
- **Scene detection** — sensitivity, minimum gap and marker colour

## File Browser

Hover skim for video and audio, filmstrip previews and their quality, grouping by date, auto-refresh, recent files, visible columns, thumbnail size, default sort and type filter, and pinned folders.

## Export

Default audio sample rate and export folder; what to do when the file already exists (ask, overwrite, or pick a new name); **Parallel render jobs**; open the folder after export; and a **Post-export command** to run on the finished file.

## Proxy

Generate proxies on import, switch to them while scrubbing, the proxy resolution, and the proxy cache folder. **Open Cache Manager…** manages them.

## Media & Cache

Cache locations, the smart render cache, the maximum cache size, thumbnail and filmstrip resolution, skim transcode quality and preview height, the size of each cache with a **Clear** for each, **Scan & Clean** for orphaned cache files, and **Clear all**.

## Colour

A calibration LUT for the viewer, the SDR viewer transfer (sRGB or BT.1886), LUT interpolation (trilinear or tetrahedral), scope refresh rate and sampling quality, the printer point size, the vectorscope's skin angle, and snapping to ports in the node graph.

## Titles

The default font, size, colour, alignment and duration of new titles.

## Timeline

Clip colours by role, clip gap, trim handle size, overview bar, minimum and maximum track height, text track height, and the selection border width.

## Viewer

Action-safe and title-safe sizes, zebra thresholds, focus-peaking sensitivity and colour, the false-colour palette editor, grid colour, checkerboard tile size, transform handle size, and the fullscreen overlay timeout.

## External Tools

The image, audio and video editors to open a clip in, and whether to reload a clip when it is edited outside.

## Accounts

Sign in to **Freesound**, so the Library's sound search can download originals.

## AI

Turn speech transcription, subject detection and screen text (OCR) on or off, see each one's status, download speech and OCR models, transcribe on the CPU, and **Connect an assistant**: check that Klipp's MCP program is installed and connected, and copy the configuration an assistant needs. See [AI Tools](../ai-tools/overview.md) and [Assistant](../ai-tools/assistant.md).

## Advanced

How long notifications stay, the performance overlay, debug logging level, and resets: window layout, recent projects, or every preference.

## About

The version and build, licence, detected hardware (CPU, GPU, memory, audio) and the technology Klipp is built on.
