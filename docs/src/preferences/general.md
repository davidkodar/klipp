# General Preferences

Open preferences with **Edit → Preferences...** (**Ctrl+,**). The pages are listed on the left, and a search box finds a setting by name. This page covers **General**; [Other Pages](./pages.md) covers the rest.

## Updates

- **Check automatically** — checks for new releases once a day
- **Include preview releases**
- **Installed version**, and **Check for updates…** to check now

## Startup

- **On launch** — what Klipp shows when it starts: the Project Manager (welcome screen), an empty project, or the last project reopened

## Project

- **Auto-save interval** — off, every 1, 5 (the default), 10 or 30 minutes, or a custom interval
- **Also auto-save every N operations**
- **Backup versions to keep**
- **Projects root** — where new projects go, and the folder the welcome screen lists

## Inspector

- **Remember collapsed sections** — keep the inspector's sections open or closed as you left them
