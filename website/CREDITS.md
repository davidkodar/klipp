# Demo media credits

The homepage film is the Klipp showcase, cut, titled, graded, mixed, narrated and exported in Klipp. The screenshots show the Seaside Day demo project. The full list with links is on the site's credits page (`credits.html`).

- Music: [“Learn to Fly (Instrumental Version)” by Josh Woodward](https://www.jamendo.com/track/628971), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Keep this attribution with any reuse of the film; on YouTube, include it in the video description.
- Sound effect: [“Whoosh stereo light (transition)” by xkeril](https://freesound.org/people/xkeril/sounds/701104/), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Narration: Klipp's built-in narration with the Kokoro model (Apache-2.0).
- Footage: 17 clips from [Coverr](https://coverr.co/license) and [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree), used under their free licences; each clip is listed on the credits page. No creator endorsement is implied.
