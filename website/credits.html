The showcase film

Behind the picture.

The film was cut, titled, graded, mixed, narrated and exported in Klipp; the screen recordings show the real Klipp interface. To fit GitHub Pages, the web copy is encoded again from Klipp’s 4K export, at 1080p and 60 frames per second.

Music

“Learn to Fly (Instrumental Version)” by Josh Woodward — released under CC BY 3.0. Mixed under the narration, with fades and adjusted loudness. Keep this attribution with any reuse of the film; on YouTube, include it in the video description.

Sound effect

“Whoosh stereo light (transition)” by xkeril on Freesound — CC0.

Narration

Spoken by Klipp’s built-in narration, using the Kokoro text-to-speech model (Apache-2.0).
